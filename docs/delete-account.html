Delete Your Account

How to permanently remove your Spun account and everything tied to it.

Applies to Spun for iOS & Android Contact spun.app.developer@gmail.com
01

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Spun and go to your Profile (tap the profile icon).
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

Your account and everything tied to it is deleted immediately — there's no waiting period, and no confirmation email is required.

02

Don't have the app installed?

Email spun.app.developer@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject line "Delete my account." Include your Spun username if you remember it.

Response time

Requests sent this way are handled manually and completed within 7 days.

03

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

Profile Username, display name, bio, email, password, and profile photo
Activity Every rating, review, listen log, list, favorite, and "listen later" save
Social graph Your follows, followers, likes, and any recommendations you sent or received
Safety records Blocks you placed and reports you filed

Nothing is retained. There's no backup copy kept and no additional retention period — deletion happens directly in our production database, not on a delay or queue.
