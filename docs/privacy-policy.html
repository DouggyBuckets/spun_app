Privacy Policy

What we collect when you rate, review, and follow music — and how to make us forget it.

Effective September 13, 2026 Applies to Spun for iOS & Android Contact spun.app.developer@gmail.com
01

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your username, email address, and password. Your password is stored as a salted bcrypt hash — we never store or see it in plain text.

Anything you choose to add to your profile — display name, bio, and profile photo — is stored so it can be shown to other users.

We store the activity you generate using the app:

  • Ratings, written reviews, and listen logs for albums and songs
  • Lists, favorites, and "listen later" saves
  • Likes, follows, and recommendations you send to other users
  • Reports you file, and the reason you give, if you report a user or a review

We don't request your location, contacts, or any device identifiers beyond what's technically required to run the app and keep you signed in.

02

How we use it

Your data runs the features you'd expect: your profile, your ratings feeding album and song averages, your feed showing activity from people you follow, and search finding other listeners.

When you rate or review something, we match it against public catalog data (titles, artists, cover art) from the Spotify Web API — this is a one-way lookup of music metadata, not a connection to any Spotify account of yours.

When you or someone else files a safety report, we use it to review the account or review in question and to enforce blocks.

No ads, no tracking

Spun shows no advertising and runs no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it to build advertising profiles — there's nowhere for that data to go.

03

Who we share it with

A small number of infrastructure providers process data on our behalf, strictly to run the app:

Cloudinary Hosts profile photos you upload.
Fly.io & Neon Host the app's server and database.
Spotify Web API Supplies public music catalog data. No account data of yours is sent to Spotify.

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone outside the providers listed above.

04

Your choices

You can edit or remove most of what you've added — profile info, favorites, lists, reviews — directly from your profile at any time.

If someone is bothering you, you can block them (which also hides your profile from them) or report a user or a review for us to look into, both from directly within the app.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile → Delete account, confirm with your password, and your account is deleted immediately and permanently — your profile, reviews, ratings, lists, follows, and every other trace of your account. There's no waiting period and no need to email us, though you're welcome to if anything goes wrong.

05

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, it and everything tied to it is removed from our database right away — deletion isn't something we queue up or delay.

06

Children's privacy

Spun isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

07

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the effective date above. Continuing to use Spun after a change means you accept the update.

08

Contact

Questions, requests to access or delete your data, or anything else — write to spun.app.developer@gmail.com.
