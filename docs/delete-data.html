Delete Your Data

Remove specific information from Spun without deleting your whole account.

Applies to Spun for iOS & Android Contact spun.app.developer@gmail.com
01

What you can remove yourself, in the app

Each of these takes effect immediately, with no waiting period:

Display name & bio Profile → Edit profile → clear the field → Save
Favorite albums Profile → tap the × on any of your four favorite slots
Listen-later saves My Activity → tap remove next to a saved item
Lists Open a list → delete the whole list, or remove a single album from it
Follows & blocks Unfollow someone from their profile, or unblock from your Blocked Users list
Recommendations Delete any recommendation from your inbox
02

Reviews and ratings

Individual reviews and ratings can't yet be removed one at a time from within the app. To have a specific review or rating deleted, email spun.app.developer@gmail.com with your username and which one you'd like removed.

Response time

These requests are handled manually and completed within 7 days.

03

Want everything gone?

If you'd rather remove all of your data at once, including your reviews and ratings, delete your account entirely from Profile → Delete account. See our account deletion page for details on what that removes.
